Demo · Step 01

Establish a trusted baseline

  1. Set the sidebar language to English
  2. Click Dashboard
  3. Select account Demo Growth
  4. Click Refresh at the top right
  5. Open Accounts → Demo Growth → View positions

Expected: AAPL quantity 8 and MSFT quantity 3, isolated to Demo Growth; one gain and one loss; source flags are explicit.

English

The backend is connected. On Dashboard, select Demo Growth and refresh. We start with AAPL quantity 8 and MSFT quantity 3, with both gain and loss examples. The source flags are explicit: the default demo says MOCK rather than pretending generated prices are live. Open Accounts and View positions for Demo Growth to confirm the same account-level quantities. Remember AAPL at 8; that is our baseline.

中文

左下角显示后端已连接。在仪表盘选择 Demo Growth 并刷新。当前 AAPL 数量是 8，MSFT 是 3，同时有盈利和亏损示例。行情来源明确标记为 MOCK，不会把生成价格冒充实时数据。进入账户页面，查看 Demo Growth 的持仓，可以确认相同的账户级数量。请记住 AAPL 的基线是 8。

Demo · Step 02

Book one share of AAPL

  1. Click Activity in the sidebar
  2. Select Demo Growth and side BUY
  3. Enter AAPL and click the search result
  4. Confirm Verified: AAPL appears
  5. Enter quantity 1 and price 100
  6. Keep the current execution time and click Book BUY trade

Expected: “AAPL BUY trade booked successfully.” appears, with a new BOOKED row at the top of the ledger.

English

Open Activity, select Demo Growth, and choose BUY. Type AAPL, then select the search result; the form requires “Verified: AAPL,” so the ticker is not an arbitrary string. Enter quantity 1 and price 100, keep the current execution time, and click Book BUY trade. The success message appears and a new BOOKED row is added to the ledger. Execution time records when the trade happened; operation time records when the system saved it.

中文

进入交易活动，选择 Demo Growth 和 BUY。输入 AAPL 后必须点击搜索结果，界面出现“Verified: AAPL”才能提交，所以股票代码不是任意字符串。数量输入 1，价格输入 100，保留当前成交时间，然后点击 Book BUY trade。成功提示出现，账本顶部新增一条 BOOKED 记录。成交时间表示交易发生时间，操作时间表示系统保存时间。

Demo · Step 03

The number changes. The evidence remains.

  1. Accounts → Demo Growth → View positions
  2. Confirm AAPL moves from 8 → 9
  3. Open Activity and click Delete on the new AAPL row
  4. Accept the browser confirmation dialog
  5. Observe CANCELLED / DELETED on the retained row
  6. View positions again and confirm AAPL 9 → 8

Key result: deletion changes the effective business facts, so the position recovers, but the ledger row remains as audit evidence.

Then return to Dashboard and click Refresh to see market value and P&L recalculate with the restored quantity.

English

Return to Demo Growth positions. AAPL moves from 8 to 9 because the new BOOKED trade is now included in replay. Go back to Activity, delete that new row, and confirm. The row is not physically removed; it remains as CANCELLED with audit reason DELETED. View positions again and AAPL returns from 9 to 8. The effective number changes, but the evidence remains. Refresh Dashboard and P&L recalculates from the restored quantity.

中文

回到 Demo Growth 持仓，AAPL 从 8 变成 9，因为新的 BOOKED 交易已经参与重放。再回到交易活动，删除刚才新增的记录并确认。记录不会被物理删除，而是保留为 CANCELLED，审计原因是 DELETED。再次查看持仓，AAPL 从 9 恢复为 8。有效数字发生变化，但证据仍然保留。刷新仪表盘后，盈亏也会按恢复后的数量重新计算。

Demo · Step 04

When quotes fail, the system stays honest

  1. Click Market Data in the sidebar
  2. Select account Demo Growth
  3. Click Refresh AAPL and point out the source flags
  4. Finnhub demo: click Simulate outage
  5. Refresh AAPL again and observe CACHED / STALE
  6. Refresh Dashboard, then return and click Restore provider

Default Mock: explain the MOCK label and skip outage controls.
Finnhub demo: normal is FINNHUB / LIVE; during outage the retained price is explicitly CACHED / STALE.

English

Open Market Data and refresh AAPL. In the default demo, the page explicitly says MOCK and not live market data. In Finnhub demo mode, a normal quote shows FINNHUB and LIVE. Simulate an outage and refresh again: a retained Redis quote is shown as CACHED and STALE, including a warning on Dashboard. Without cache, the API returns a clear 503; it never switches silently to Mock or invents a zero price. Finally, restore the provider.

中文

进入市场数据并刷新 AAPL。默认演示会明确显示 MOCK，并说明不是实时行情。在 Finnhub 演示模式下，正常报价显示 FINNHUB 和 LIVE。模拟故障后再次刷新，Redis 中保留的报价会明确标记为 CACHED 和 STALE，仪表盘也会显示警告。如果没有缓存，接口会返回清晰的 503，不会偷偷切换到 Mock，也不会编造零价格。最后恢复数据源。
